What we stand for

Three commitments, one heart.

We Share Foundation LBG is licensed by Ghana's Non‑Profit Organisation Secretariat under the Department of Social Welfare. Everything we do sits under three objects set out in our founding documents.

Object 01

Charity & Advocacy

Direct relief and a voice for communities who are too often spoken over rather than listened to.

Object 02

Care for the Aged

Home visits, companionship and support for elderly members of our communities, the walking figures in our own emblem.

Object 03

Empowerment

Practical support, school supplies, essentials and opportunity for young people and families working to get ahead.

On the ground

Recent programs & visits.

From sandy compounds under the mango tree to school gates, our team shows up in person because humanity is best shared face to face.

Community outreach

Door‑to‑door & community visits

Our volunteers meet families and elders where they are — under the community tree, in the compound, at the school gate — to listen first and support second.

School Supply Drive

Backpacks, books and stationery donated to students in need nationwide.

Essentials & item donations
